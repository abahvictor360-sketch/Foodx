## Upwork / Fiverr / Contra portfolio entry

**Title:** 3D Bakery & Coffee Shop Website: Next.js, Three.js, WhatsApp Ordering

**Description:**

> A mobile-first website for Foodx, a bakery and coffee bar in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos. The hero features an interactive 3D iced coffee built with React Three Fiber, where customers switch between drinks and watch the colours change live. A scroll-driven 3D section walks visitors through the menu, and liquid-glass UI panels keep the brand feeling premium.
>
> Customers can browse 20+ items by category, add to cart, and check out with delivery or pickup. The order arrives on the shop's WhatsApp, so there's no payment gateway or backend to maintain.
>
> I optimised it for phones, tablets and desktop: 3D scenes pause off-screen, touch devices scroll natively, and text contrast is tuned so copy stays readable over photos and 3D.
>
> **Deliverables:** UI design, 3D scenes, frontend development, product photo background removal, responsive QA, Vercel deployment.
